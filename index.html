How In-Browser Stream Remuxing Works

Why traditional online converters take 10+ minutes while NanoMOV converts your video in seconds.

1. Stream Remuxing vs. Transcoding

Standard converters decode every single pixel and re-encode with lossy compression. NanoMOV simply rewrites the container wrapper atoms (`ftyp`, `moov`, `mdat`) and copies raw H.264/AAC packets, finishing in seconds with zero CPU bottleneck.

2. Web FastStart (+faststart) Optimization

Apple QuickTime files often place the metadata (`moov` box) at the end of the file. NanoMOV relocates `moov` to the beginning of the file, allowing immediate playback on iPhones, Android, Chrome, and social networks without waiting for full download.

3. Absolute Privacy & Zero Uploads

Your video never touches a cloud server. Everything executes locally in your browser memory via WebAssembly and pure client-side binary streams, protecting your private recordings from third-party storage.
